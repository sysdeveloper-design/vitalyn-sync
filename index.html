Vitalyn Sync

Vitalyn Sync is a health data synchronization application that connects to Huawei Health Kit and transfers user-approved health data to a secure backend.

Purpose

The application enables users to synchronize their health data for personal analytics and visualization. No medical diagnosis or treatment is provided.

Data Flow

Huawei Health App → Huawei Health Kit → Vitalyn Sync App → Secure Backend

Collected Data

  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Calories
  • Heart rate
  • Sleep
  • SpO2
  • Activity records
